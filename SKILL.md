# Diagnose Before You Respond

You do not need to install anything to try this once. The lines between the dashes at the very top are just this file's label; leave them in. On GitHub, copy this using the **Raw** button near the top of the page rather than selecting the rendered text, so the tables and links below paste in cleanly. Send the whole file as your first message in any AI chat tool, then paste in the complaint.

A complaint is a diagnosis problem before it is a reply problem. Answering only the surface wording either leaves the real concern unaddressed, or over-explains a simple issue that never needed it.

## Gather the Inputs

- The complaint as written or reported
- Whatever is known about what actually happened
- Whether this is the first complaint of its kind from this person, if known

## Identify What Is Actually Driving It

Before drafting a reply, work out which of these is the closest fit:

- `genuine-fault`: the stated issue is real and is the actual problem, nothing more is going on underneath it
- `feeling-unheard`: the surface issue is real but small; the real driver is that nobody acknowledged it until the complaint was made
- `expectation-mismatch`: the customer expected something that was never actually promised
- `pattern-signal`: this echoes a complaint raised before, worth checking whether it is one incident or a recurring issue

Do not default to `feeling-unheard` or `pattern-signal` just because they sound more insightful. A genuine, simple fault is common, and treating it as something deeper adds a response nobody asked for.

## Draft the Reply

Address the actual driver identified above, not just the surface wording. If the driver is `genuine-fault`, fix the fault and say so plainly, do not add an unnecessary explanation about being heard. If the driver is `feeling-unheard`, acknowledge the delay or silence directly, not just the original issue. End with one clear next step.

## Apply the Guardrails

- Never invent a cause the evidence does not support
- Never promise a refund, replacement, or compensation as already done; propose it for approval
- Do not turn a simple, genuine fault into a longer response than it needs

## Stop When the Task Is Unsafe

Do not produce a confident diagnosis when:

- The complaint is too vague to distinguish a genuine fault from something else
- The request is to draft a reply that avoids acknowledging a fault the evidence actually confirms

## Require Human Review

This drafts a reply and proposes the diagnosis. Sending anything, and any compensation offered, stays subject to explicit human approval.
